The completion ring counts cards owned, not cards listed

Zach: "it shows 97% complete with only 3 missing cards but actually I am
missing 97 cards."

Measured on his Tony Stark deck: 97 cards listed against a target of 100, so
the ring read 97%. He owns THREE of them. He had just imported the list, and
DeckList was computing completion from total_cards -- what the list SAYS --
rather than from what is in the binder.

This is the wrong-record failure in its purest form. The screen reported a deck
ready to play while 94 of its cards did not exist in his collection. A missing
entry costs a tap; this costs a recount against cardboard.

GET /api/decks now returns owned_cards, using the same rule as the deck view
(utils/deckIdentity.js): exact printing AND finish, collection rows only, minus
copies already claimed by a higher-priority requirement, capped at what the
deck needs. Verified against the real dev database -- the new figure agrees
with the deck detail view on every deck, which is the number Zach trusts.

Two wrong answers before that one, both caught by measuring rather than
reasoning: the table is `collection`, not `user_collection`; and
deck_card_allocations is legacy -- routes/decks.js only ever DELETEs from it
and dev holds zero rows, so reading it would have reported 0% for every deck.

ALSO IN THIS COMMIT

Deck cost. The row now says "94 missing · $181.78", and a card with no cached
price contributes nothing rather than zeroing the total -- an unknown price is
not a free card, and the row says so.

Two labelled buttons in selection mode, per Zach: "maybe when selecting decks
it should be 2 buttons that show one for delete and then another for export".
A bare trash icon beside an export button is ambiguous, and one of them
destroys work. Delete is outlined, not filled: filled reads as the primary
action and it is not.

Card detail in the deck view. READ-ONLY, and that matters: CardInspectorModal
edits a COLLECTION entry by id, and a deck card's id is a deck_cards row.
Passing one straight through would PUT to /api/collection/<deck_cards.id> and
silently rewrite whichever collection row shares that number -- on a card the
user is not even looking at. The modal now refuses to save in read-only mode
rather than trusting every future caller to remember.
