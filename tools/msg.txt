Fold the card catalogue under the Scryfall source

Zach, after using the deployed Settings screen:

  "Data sources and card catalogue they weren't separated I feel like each data
   source should have a drop down showing like details of what it's syncing.
   Scryfall being cards and moxfield being decks as the example."

I had built two flat sections -- "Data sources" listing the catalogue, and
"Card catalogue" holding its rows -- so the same subject appeared twice with no
stated relationship between them. The mock draws both, and I followed the
layout without asking what the two sections were FOR.

His reading is better than mine and better than the mock's literal shape: the
catalogue rows ARE the Scryfall source's detail, so they belong under it,
revealed by tapping. Scryfall syncs CARDS; Moxfield will sync DECKS.

It is also the shape that extends. Moxfield becomes a second source with its
own detail rows rather than needing another top-level section -- which is
exactly what the flat layout could not have absorbed, and the reason the
duplication would have got worse rather than stayed cosmetic.

The row now states what the source syncs ("Cards · 105,473 · nightly") instead
of only naming it, and the chevron ROTATES when open. That last part is not
decoration: a row that expands with no visual change is the failure this
project keeps hitting -- it renders, it works, and nothing tells you it did
anything.

Moxfield stays absent until the integration exists.
