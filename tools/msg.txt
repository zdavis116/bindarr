...AND THE COLLECTION FILTER SHEET, at 121.

That is the sixth, seventh and eighth control on this project that rendered,
was wired correctly, passed every test, and could not be used -- three of them
in one session, after I had already named this as my blind spot. So it stops
being something to remember:

  - frontend/src/utils/zLayers.js gives the stacking order names and reasons.
    Bare numbers scattered through JSX cannot be reasoned about; these can.
  - fixedSurfaceStacking.test.js reads the nav's z-index FROM THE STYLESHEET
    and fails any fixed bottom-anchored surface below it.

That guard immediately found six MORE, all pre-existing and none mine:
CardInspectorModal, AdminPanel, SharedCollection and three in DeckBuilder, all
at 999 -- one below the nav. 999 looks like "on top of everything" and is not.
CardInspectorModal is where a card's quantity and condition are edited, so this
was not cosmetic.

What the guard cannot do: it reads z-index numbers, not rendered geometry. It
cannot see below-the-fold, overflow:hidden clipping, or overlap. Only a person
holding a phone catches those, which is still how every one of these was found.
