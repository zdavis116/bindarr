Parse real Moxfield exports: DFC names, prefixed numbers, foil markers

Zach's 86-line Iron Man list, pasted WITH set codes and collector numbers,
still lost cards. Three separate faults in the parser, each of which loses a
card silently: the line parses, so it is never reported as unreadable -- it
simply never finds a card.

Measured against his real catalogue: 86 of 86 lines now resolve exactly. Before
these fixes, three did not.

1. DOUBLE-FACED CARDS. "1 Tony Stark / The Invincible Iron Man (MSH) 80"

   This was his COMMANDER -- the one card a Commander deck cannot do without --
   and it matched nothing. card_cache stores modal DFCs under the front face
   alone ('Tony Stark', msh #80, layout modal_dfc). Moxfield writes ONE slash;
   Scryfall's own convention is TWO, and 931 cards in this catalogue use it.
   Neither form was handled.

   The name now reduces to the front face, which is the identity the catalogue
   stores and does not depend on the exporter spelling the back face our way.

2. SET-PREFIXED COLLECTOR NUMBERS. "1 Master Transmuter (PLST) CON-31"

   The List numbers its reprints CON-31. The matcher only understood digits, so
   the number came back undefined AND "CON-31" stayed welded to the name,
   producing "Master Transmuter CON-31" -- unmatchable however good the rest of
   the pipeline is.

3. Foil markers and commas in names were already handled, and now have tests so
   they stay that way -- "1 Rescue, Pepper Potts (MSC) 755 *F*" exercises both
   at once.

TESTS: deckTextParse.test.js, DT-TC1 to DT-TC6, every case a literal line from
the file Zach sent. Three verified by reintroducing the bug -- the commander
lost to slash handling, CON-31 welded to the name, and the general form where
any parsed name keeps a digit from its collector number.

343 tests pass, lint and build clean.
