Say when decks want more copies than exist

Zach searched Tony Stark from a THIRD deck and saw "1 owned, in other decks".
He was right that the wording was poor, and the reason is worse than vagueness.

MEASURED ON DEV:

    owned         1     one physical Tony Stark
    in_deck_qty   2     'Test' (commander) AND 'Test 2' (mainboard)
    available     0

TWO decks had claimed ONE card. The old line implied a tidy situation -- "it's
in another deck, go get it" -- when in fact his decks promise more copies than
he owns. Sleeve Test and Test 2 is silently short, discovered at the table,
against cardboard. A missing card costs a tap; a wrong record costs a recount.

This state is REACHABLE BY DESIGN now: the permissive rules deliberately let a
second deck claim a spoken-for copy, because refusing was the thing that broke
deck building. Which means the screen has to say it, since nothing else will.
Removing an enforcement without adding the report is how a system gets quietly
wrong -- the same trap as OFF_COLOUR earlier in this branch.

WORDING now states both numbers rather than summarising them:

    2 of 3 free                          copies genuinely usable here
    1 owned, used in 1 deck              none free, but the books balance
    Only 1 owned, but 2 decks want it    the books do NOT balance

"in 2 decks" is actionable; "in other decks" is not.

ALSO FIXED: in_deck_qty counts every deck INCLUDING the one on screen, so a
card already added here was reported as being somewhere else as well. The
`elsewhere` helper subtracts this deck's own claim.
