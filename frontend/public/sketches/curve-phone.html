This one actually works

Same behaviour as the desktop prototype, reworked for a thumb:

  • Tap a coloured block → filters the list to those cards.
  • Tap a role chip → isolates that role across every bar. Try Ramp: you can see instantly that yours stops at mana value 3.
  • Tap a turn row → filters to what is castable by then.
  • With ramp / Lands only → recomputes the turn numbers.
  • Tap a card → the sheet. Hover does not exist on a phone, so the full rules text lives here instead of in a tooltip.
  • Change role in the sheet → pick a different one; it sticks, the chart redraws, and the card is marked as yours not Scryfall's.

What is different from the desktop, and why

  • No hover. Every hover became a tap-to-open sheet. The gist line under each card name carries what the tooltip used to.
  • Rows are 48px. Thumb targets, not mouse targets.
  • The turn view is always visible, not behind a toggle — you said you would rather have more information than less.
  • Filters show a clear bar rather than relying on you remembering what you tapped, because on a phone the chart scrolls off screen.

Roles come from Scryfall

Every role here is derived from the oracle_tags bulk file — 95% agreement, measured — and each card's sheet names the exact tag it matched. One tap overrides it permanently, and an overridden card says "your choice" instead of a tag.
