Why this shape. The old screen modelled physical pockets — page 3, slot 7 — which is why it needed renumber warnings, capacity maths and a page navigator. You said a container is a bucket you find things in by their order, so the sort stack IS the whole model: no pockets, nothing to renumber. One control, three screens — the same stack drives the desktop groups, the phone list, and the File-all action.

What this drops on purpose. Pocket positions, page spreads, capacity warnings, renumbering, and per-compartment filing rules — all of it existed to model a binder as physical geometry. What it keeps: containers, a card's location, unsorted, and bulk filing.

Open question I could not answer for you. Whether the stack is per container (Binder sorts by Type, Bulk Box by Set) or one global preference. The mockup shows per-container with an "Apply to container" button, because you already keep a binder and a bulk box for different reasons.
